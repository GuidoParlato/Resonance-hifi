# Resonance

A Mac app that plays your own music collection on your hi-fi.

Point it at a folder of music (on your Mac, an external drive, or a NAS mounted in Finder),
pick a network streamer, and play whole albums, gapless, with volume control from your Mac.
It works with UPnP / DLNA and OpenHome renderers: most network streamers and amplifiers,
many smart TVs, and the BubbleUPnP app on Android.

No cloud, no account, no internet needed. Your music never leaves your home network.

## Download

**[Get the latest version →](https://github.com/GuidoParlato/Resonance-hifi/releases/latest)**

1. Download **Resonance.zip** and unzip it.
2. Drag **Resonance** into your Applications folder and open it.
3. macOS will say it can't check the app. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.
4. Allow **Local Network** access when asked. Resonance needs it to find your streamer.

Needs macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
